Confirmed business rules

  • Self-submit only — employee cannot create a claim for someone else.
  • Expense Approver is locked to Line Manager from Employee master (expense_approver).
  • Any Reject requires a mandatory reason logged in Activities (Comment).
  • Vendor-paid purchases → Purchase Invoice, not Expense Claim.
